  1. 01

    Planning

    We clarify the question behind the job and set the area, resolution and date. Legal requirements and neighbours are checked before the flight.

  2. 02

    Flight

    The drone flies the grid automatically. On the ground we see live which areas are covered, and re-fly any gaps on the spot.

  3. 03

    Processing

    Every image gets its exact position from post-processing against our own base station. From that we produce the orthophoto, elevation model and findings.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    You receive the data in your formats and in the official reference system, with a short report explaining what the map shows and how accurate it is.

Every image is counted three times.

The GNSS receiver, flight controller and camera each count every trigger independently. If the three counts disagree, the dataset is discarded and re-flown. That way every image position can be proven.
